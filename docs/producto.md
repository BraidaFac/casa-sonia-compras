# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Purchasing staff at Casa Sonia. Internal tool, used on desktop at an office or store. Users navigate many SKUs per session and need fast, low-error order entry.

## Product Purpose

Single-purpose purchase order creation system that integrates with Odoo 19 ERP. Lets staff build orders article by article (supplier, colors, sizes, quantities), generate PDFs, and sync images to Google Drive.

## Brand Personality

Precise, efficient, trustworthy. Dark-mode-native tool for focused work — not a consumer product, not SaaS marketing.

## Anti-references

- Pastel/cream SaaS dashboards (Notion, Linear light mode)
- Consumer e-commerce brightness
- Heavy decoration / glassmorphism for its own sake

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over ceremony** — every interaction should require minimum clicks; common paths should feel effortless.
2. **Data density, not data clutter** — show what's needed, hide what isn't.
3. **Dark is the native habitat** — amber accent on warm near-black is the identity; don't fight it.
4. **Error prevention over error recovery** — clear affordances reduce mis-selections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Keyboard navigable. Shift-click range selection for size chips (already implemented).
